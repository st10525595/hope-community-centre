Our Story Since 2018

Built By The Community,
For The Community

What started as a small winter drive is now a registered NPO supporting 200+ families every month.

💚

Our Mission

To restore dignity by feeding families, educating children and skilling youth in Mbombela and surrounding townships.

👁️

Our Vision

A Mbombela where no child goes to school hungry and every young person has a skill to earn a living.

🤝

Our Values

Dignity, love, community and accountability. 100% of donations go directly to the community.

Our Journey

2018: Winter food drive with 20 parcels.
2020: Started after-school tutoring.
2022: Registered NPO + Youth Skills Workshop.
2026: 200+ families monthly, 80+ children tutored, 20 volunteers.
